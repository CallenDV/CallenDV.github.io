the cheese spot

recipe: bacon jalepeño poppers

cooking statistics

serves 24. takes 35 minutes.

ingredients

  • 8oz block cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 cup shredded pepper jack
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • kosher salt
  • freshly cracked black pepper
  • 12 jalepeños
  • 12 slices bacon, halved

instructions

  1. preheat oven to 400 fahrenheit. in a large bowl, combine cream cheese, pepper jack, and garlic, season with salt and pepper.
  2. halve jalepeños lengthwise, then use a spoon to remove seeds and veins. fill with cheese mix and wrap each with a half slice of bacon.
  3. place on a baking sheet and bake until bacon is crispy and peppers are soft, 25 minutes.
